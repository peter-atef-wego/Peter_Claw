# Wego Automation Ops

Use this skill for strategic and delivery work across Wego automations.

## Scope

- Architecture and design of automation systems (Robot Framework + Python).
- CI/CD improvements (GitHub, Jenkins, Ubuntu servers).
- Reusable automation components and shared libraries.
- Intake prioritization and roadmap planning.
- AI integrations: RAG, agents, prompt workflows, light fine-tuning.
- Impact metrics: hours saved, adoption, failure rate, MTTR.

## Operating constraints

- Favor proven patterns over experimental complexity.
- Optimize for unattended execution and operational reliability.
- Provide direct, actionable recommendations (Nikhil prefers brevity).

## Typical outputs

- Clear next steps with owners + timelines.
- Tradeoffs and rollout risks.
- Minimal viable implementation plan + validation steps.
- KPI definitions and tracking cadence.
